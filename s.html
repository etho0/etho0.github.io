Mini App Sensor Side-Channel

Telegram delegates accelerometer; gyroscope; magnetometer; device-orientation to every Mini App iframe. On Android these require no permission, no prompt and no gesture — the stream starts the instant the page loads.

This measures what an attacker actually receives: achieved sample rate, and whether discrete physical taps are recoverable from the signal. Tap inference is the basis of the published TouchLogger / ACCessory PIN-recovery work; Gyrophone showed gyroscopes can even recover speech.

Nothing is clicked to start this. Put the phone down, then tap the screen or the desk a few times and watch the detector.
