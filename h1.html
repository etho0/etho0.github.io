H1 · Bridge origin binding

This measures it directly. Stage 1 proves the bridge answers on the origin the app was opened at. It then navigates the frame to a different origin — exactly the "open redirect / partner callback" case the fix describes. Stage 2 asks whether the bridge still answers there.

Replies on stage 2 ⇒ no binding ⇒ a document that replaces the Mini App inherits the bot's bridge.
